        Nothing is copied or deleted while you review. Keep marks a frame to be copied to the Mac at the Import step; leave and reject both leave it out of the import, but only a reject is fine to lose — the Format card step warns before formatting would lose a frame you chose to leave. Nothing in this app ever deletes from the card. Decisions are saved as you make them and are still there after a restart.
