Keys

K
keep, and move on
X
skip, and move on
1 – 5
keep at that rating, and move on
0
keep, no rating, and move on
R
also keep the RAF for this frame
← →
back and forward without deciding
U
undo this frame's decision
G / Esc
back to the grid

Nothing is copied or deleted here. Keep marks a frame to be copied into the library when you press Import on the card page; skip leaves it out of the import. Nothing in this app ever deletes from the card — it is only emptied when you format it in the camera, and the card page says when that is safe. Decisions are saved as you make them and are still there after a restart.
